## Findings

### 1. [medium] Customer logins see who staff assigned to their disputes and promises, through the audit trail
- `DisputeService.setAssignees` writes `DISPUTE_ASSIGNEES_CHANGED` on entity `DISPUTE` with before/after `{"assignees":["USER:<id>","ROLE:CUSTOMER:COLLECTION_POC",...]}`. `PaymentPromiseService.setAssignees` writes `PROMISE_ASSIGNEES_CHANGED` the same way.
- A customer login's trail keeps these rows:
  - `AuditController.CUSTOMER_READABLE` includes PROMISE, and `AuditTimelineService.related` takes in the customer's own (non-internal) disputes on INVOICE, PAYMENT and CUSTOMER with `includeRelated`.
  - `AuditTimelineService.withoutPocIdentity` (audit/AuditTimelineService.java:302-319) drops only actions containing "POC", CUSTOMER-level `CHANGE_*` rows and non-shared documents. `stripPoc` removes JSON field *names* containing "poc" plus `STAFF_NAME_FIELDS` (:455-456). The field `assignees` is not among them, so the staff user ids and role tokens pass through untouched.
- This contradicts the slice's own hiding elsewhere: `DisputeDto.assignees` and `PromiseDto.assignees` are staff-only, and `assigneeUserId` is `pocRestricted`. Assignment is internal work (D17: tasks are removed entirely from the customer trail).
- Scenario: staff `PUT /api/promises/{id}/assignees` with `[USER:12]`. The customer login calls `GET /api/audit?entityType=PROMISE&entityId={id}` and gets a `PROMISE_ASSIGNEES_CHANGED` row whose afterJson is `{"assignees":["USER:12"]}`. The same happens on its invoice trail with `includeRelated=true` for its own dispute.
- Fix: in `withoutPocIdentity`, drop `*_ASSIGNEES_CHANGED` rows (or add `assignees` to the hidden fields, after which before≠after would still leave an empty row, so dropping is cleaner). Add an assertion to AssigneeApiTest.

### 2. [medium] As of a date, a record-level role assignee on a dispute or promise resolves to nobody (B3)
- `DisputeController.detail` and `PaymentPromiseController.detail` call `AssigneeService.describeAt` when `AsOf.current() != null`. For a RECORD-level role, `describeAt` calls `recordPoc(type, recordId, role)` (assignee/AssigneeService.java:203-209), which handles only INVOICE and PAYMENT (`default -> Optional.empty()`).
- `SALES_POC@RECORD` on an invoice dispute, `COLLECTION_POC@RECORD` on a payment dispute, and `COLLECTION_POC@RECORD` on a promise therefore always show `unresolved` with no people in a past view. The live view resolves them correctly: AssigneeApiTest asserts the promise's own Collection POC live.
- The implementer disclosed this as a gap, but it breaks B3 ("as-of reads for every list/report"). It is a small fix:
  - DISPUTE: read `Dispute` at T and take its target's POC at T;
  - PROMISE: `records.find(PaymentPromise.class, id).map(PaymentPromise::getCollectionPoc)`.
- No test covers the as-of detail of either record.

### 3. [low] The customer's dispute list and export accept the new staff-only `assigneeUserId` and `internal` filters
- `DisputeController.list` (dispute/DisputeController.java:70-71) and `export` (:147-149) parse and run against the full `TableSchemas.DISPUTES`, not `DISPUTES.visibleTo(currentUser.isCustomer())`. `PaymentPromiseController.schema()` does it correctly.
- Both columns are new in this slice (TableSchemas.java:369-375). A customer login can send `filter=assigneeUserId:eq:12`, or `:is_not_empty`, and learn from `totalElements` which staff user is assigned to each of its own disputes. This is the same information finding 1 exposes. Disclosed as a gap.
- Fix: use `DISPUTES.visibleTo(currentUser.isCustomer())` in `list` and `export`.

### 4. [low] CREATE_DISPUTE and CREATE_PROMISE work out who to notify inside the customer lock window (§5.10.6)
- §5.10.6: "Inside the lock window there are only: the dedupe re-check, the guard query, the domain write … and the effect row. Rendering, holder resolution, `principalFor` for probes … all happen before."
- `CreateDisputeHandler.perform` (automation/action/CreateDisputeHandler.java:141-148) and `CreatePromiseHandler.perform` (:212-213) call `AssigneeService.notifyAssignedTo` after the write. That runs `EmailTargets.findUnscoped`, then `recipients`/`resolve`: per-holder `regionAccess.holds` and `visibilityProbe.canSee`, which calls `principalFor`, for every role holder, all while `ActionExecutor.act` holds the customer row lock (ActionExecutor.java:143-149).
- Concurrent saves on that customer wait on it (A5: never slow the save). There is no statement-count test like TaskActionTest's 30-statement bound. Disclosed as a gap.
- Fix: role holders on the target record could be resolved in `prepare`, as CREATE_TASK does, and passed to `notifyAssigned` as `recipients`.

### 5. [low] "No Collection POC" beats "Nothing owed", and preview and execution disagree about nothing owed
- `ActionExecutor.act` throws on any `prepare` problem before `perform` (ActionExecutor.java:142). `CreatePromiseHandler.prepare` still adds "No Collection POC" (:139). The ≤ 0 check was moved into `perform` (:199).
- Result: a paid invoice (balance 0) on a customer with no Collection POC records a FAILED effect and a FAILED run, not the SKIPPED "Nothing owed" of §5.10.5. On an INVOICE `ENTITY_UPDATED` rule this happens every time a payment settles such an invoice.
- Separately, `preview` puts "Nothing owed" into `problems` (:176), so the preview says the action cannot run while execution treats it as a harmless skip.
- Fix: check the amount first and return `Skipped` before the POC problem is raised (for example, compute the amount in `prepare` and skip the POC resolution when it is ≤ 0). Report it in preview as a warning.
