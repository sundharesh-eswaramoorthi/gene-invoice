## Findings

### 1. [medium] Role holders who cannot open the task are notified and listed as its people (PC-2, §5.9.1)
- **Where:** `B/assignee/AssigneeService.java:127-132` (`resolve`) and `:236` (`notifyAssigned`).
- **What happens:** a ROLE token resolves to every seat or record-POC holder who has the owner's VIEW privilege in the region (`regionAccess.holds`). Nothing checks the book. §5.9.1, however, says notifyAssigned reaches "resolved, *visible*, active people", and PC-2's stated aim is that nobody is pointed at a link they would get 403 or 404 on.
- **Probe A:** colin is a COLLECTION_POC with NORTH READ/BOOK and holds Acme's Collection seat. The admin creates a task on sid's Acme invoice, assigned to `COLLECTION_POC@CUSTOMER`. Result: `notified=1 link=/tasks/1 colinGetStatus=404 people=[{"userId":7,"name":"COLIN.COLLECTIONS","primary":true}]`.
- **Consequence:** colin gets a "Task assigned" notification whose link returns 404. The task page shows him as the person the role means, while `/mine` and `canChangeStatus` (correctly) leave him out.
- **Why the tests miss it:** `MyTasksTest.aTaskOnARecordICannotSeeIsNeverListedAsMine` checks only the list, count and GET. It never checks the notification or `people`.
- **Fix:** in `resolve` and `notifyAssigned`, keep a holder only when `visibilityProbe.canSee(holder, type, id, region)` is also true (a non-throwing check). Assert no TASK_ASSIGNED for colin in that test.

### 2. [medium] The audit trail's `includeRelated` exposes tasks to staff without TASK_VIEW
- **Where:** `B/audit/AuditTimelineService.java:102,122,139,156`.
- **What happens:** for any staff caller, the related tasks of a customer, invoice or payment are added. Nothing checks TASK_VIEW.
- **Probe B:** the role is ADMIN minus TASK_*. `GET /api/tasks` gives 403, but `GET /api/audit?entityType=CUSTOMER&entityId=…&includeRelated=true` gives 200 and contains the task's title and description (`leaksTitle=true`).
- **Fix:** pass `tasks = includeRelated && !customerView && currentUser.has(TASK_VIEW)`. Better still, filter the related tasks through `forTasks` visibility, the same predicate as `TaskService.requireVisible`.

### 3. [low] `canChangeStatus` is true for an assignee on a CANCELLED task, whose only move they are refused
- **Where:** `B/task/TaskService.java:476` (`live && (canEdit || assignedToMe)`). The rule is at `:79`/`:243-246` (only TASK_MANAGE may reopen a cancelled task).
- **Probe C:** `canChangeStatus=true reopenStatus=403`.
- **Consequence:** D14 flags are meant to mirror the server rule, so the UI offers a button that always fails.
- **Fix:** add `&& t.getStatus() != CANCELLED` to the assignee branch.

### 4. [low] PATCH probes new USER assignees while holding the customer and task locks
- **Where:** `B/task/TaskService.java:182` (`lockForWrite`) and then `:193` (`assigneeService.requireVisible`).
- **What happens:** §5.9.1 "Order of work" and PC-4 say resolution and probing are read-only and happen *before* the customer lock. `create` follows that order; `patch` runs a `principalFor` plus scope query per new assignee inside the lock window.
- **Fix:** read the task's record and region unlocked, then validate and probe, then `lockForWrite` and re-check the version.

### 5. [low] Who is left out of TASK_ASSIGNED depends on the task's origin, not on who made the change
- **Where:** `B/task/TaskService.java:309`.
- **What happens:** a user who PATCHes an AUTOMATION-made task and adds themselves is notified of their own action (`except = null`). §5.9.2 says the actor is left out for a USER-origin change and the owner is included for AUTOMATION.
- **Fix:** key it on `WriteOrigins.current(currentUser).kind()` rather than `task.getOrigin()`.

### 6. [low] A task cancelled by the customer-delete cascade can be reopened
- **Probe D:** TASK_MANAGE moves a task on a deleted customer from CANCELLED to OPEN (200, `entityDeleted:true, status:OPEN`), and PATCH also succeeds. The task then counts again as open or overdue work (list, summary, `/mine/count`) on a record that no longer exists.
- **Where:** `B/task/TaskService.java:231-246` does not check `entityDeleted`.
- **Fix:** refuse writes other than viewing on an `entityDeleted` task with 400 "The record this task was on has been deleted".
